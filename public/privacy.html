Privacy Policy — ICB Shuttle Manager

Last updated: September 2026

ICB Shuttle Manager is an internal tool used by Indian Club Bahrain's badminton section to record game attendance, track shuttle usage, and manage payments between flight admins and players. This page explains what information the app collects and how it is used.

What information is collected

  • Player names, entered by a flight admin when adding a member.
  • Phone numbers, optionally added so payment reminders can be sent via WhatsApp.
  • Attendance records — which players were present or absent at each recorded game.
  • Payment records — amounts owed and amounts paid for each game.
  • Admin login details — a name and a PIN, used only to sign in. PINs are stored as one-way cryptographic hashes and are never stored or transmitted in plain text.

How this information is used

All data is used solely to operate the app's core purpose: calculating each player's share of shuttle costs, tracking who has paid, and letting flight admins and club management manage this within their own club. Player names and balances are also visible to that player themselves via a private, unguessable personal summary link.

Where data is stored

Data is stored using Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore) and Firebase Authentication, both operated by Google. The application server is hosted on Render.com. No data is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for any purpose outside this club's own administration.

WhatsApp sharing

The app can open WhatsApp with a pre-filled payment reminder message or a shared screenshot, but only when a flight admin explicitly chooses to send one. No messages are sent automatically or without the admin's action.

Data retention and deletion

Club Super Admins can erase a flight's members, games, and payment history at any time from within the app. To request full removal of your personal data, contact your flight admin or the club's Super Admin.

Children's privacy

This app is intended for use by adult club administrators managing club membership records. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be directed to Indian Club Bahrain's badminton section administration.
